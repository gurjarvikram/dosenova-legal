# Account Deletion — DoseNova

**Last updated:** 12 September 2026

**App:** DoseNova (`com.dosenova.app`)
**Operated by:** **Vijaylaxmi Gurjar**, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India
**Contact:** **dosenova01@gmail.com**

You can delete your DoseNova account and everything in it at any time. You do
not need to ask us, and you do not need a reason.

---

## Delete it in the app

1. Open DoseNova and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Delete account**.
3. Read the warning and choose **Continue**.
4. Type `DELETE` to confirm, then choose **Delete forever**.
5. Enter your password, or confirm with Google if that is how you sign in.
   Your device asks for this because deleting is irreversible and we want to
   be sure it is you.

The app signs you out and erases everything on the device immediately. The
records held on our servers are erased automatically straight afterwards.

## Delete it without the app

If you have uninstalled DoseNova, lost the device, or cannot sign in, email
**dosenova01@gmail.com** from the address on the account with the subject
**Delete my account**.

We may ask you to confirm ownership of the address before we act — an account
holds medical information, and we will not delete one on the word of somebody
who cannot show it is theirs. We complete verified requests within **30 days**,
and usually within a few days.

---

## What is deleted

Everything below is erased from our servers and from your device:

- Your account and sign-in credentials
- Medicines, dosages, schedules and reminders
- Your full dose history — taken, skipped, missed and snoozed
- Family profiles you created, and their medicines and history
- Prescriptions, medicine photos and any file you uploaded
- Stock counts and refill settings
- Your emergency medical profile, which never leaves the device in the first
  place
- Purchase records held for verifying your subscription
- Caregiver connections: a caregiver you invited loses access to your records,
  and if you were a caregiver for somebody else, your access to theirs ends

Health report PDFs you exported and shared are yours and are not ours to
delete. They are on your device, or wherever you sent them.

## What is kept, and for how long

| Data | Kept | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Analytics data tied to your account | We ask Google to delete it at the moment you delete your account. Google completes this within **72 hours**. | Deletion at Google is a request we make; it is not instantaneous. Events collected before you ever signed in carry no account identifier and expire after at most 14 months. |
| Crash reports | Up to **90 days** from when each was received, then deleted automatically. | A crash report cannot be deleted individually. When you delete your account, your identifier stops being attached to any new report, and any report still waiting on your device is discarded. |
| Backups | Up to **30 days**, then permanently erased. | Backups are written on a rolling schedule and expire on their own. They are never used to restore a deleted account. |
| Records we are required by law to keep | Only for as long as the law requires. | Tax and payment records, for example. These never include your medical data. |

Nothing in that table is used to rebuild your account or shown to anyone.

## Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account **does not cancel a Google Play subscription**, and we
cannot cancel one for you. Cancel it in the Play Store before you delete:
**Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions →
DoseNova → Cancel**.

If you delete the account first, the subscription keeps billing until you
cancel it in Play.

## Deleting cannot be undone

There is no recovery, no grace period and no archive we can restore from.
If you only want to stop using the app for a while, sign out instead — signing
out also wipes the device, and your records stay safe on our servers until you
sign back in.
